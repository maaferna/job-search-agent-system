# 0007 · Every past role is always named; text is shortened, roles are never omitted

**Status:** accepted

## Context
To fit two pages or raise keyword coverage, it was tempting to drop older or less relevant roles. Each
omission created an artificial gap in the timeline — and real gaps are already a risk for this profile.

## Decision
Every role is named in every CV. To gain space, the text of a role is shortened (e.g. several older roles
collapsed into one compressed block that still lists each one with its dates), never removed.

## Consequences
- Timelines are consistent across all documents and with the job boards' structured data.
- Space is recovered by trimming certifications and wording, not history.
